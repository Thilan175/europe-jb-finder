Verification checklist
  • Agency licence is currently valid.
  • Agency is not suspended/cancelled.
  • Vacancy/job order can be checked with SLBFE.
  • Employer, salary, duties and contract are clear.
  • Fees are confirmed with SLBFE before payment.
  • Never rely on screenshots or star ratings alone.
